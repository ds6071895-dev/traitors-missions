Mission Briefing

Boat Race

  • Ride the swell. Point down the face of a wave and you gain speed for free.
  • Launch the crests. Air time refills your boost — land flat to keep it.
  • Spin it. Hold Space in the air to trick: throttle flips, steering rolls. Let go and the hull snaps to the nearest whole turn — that is the landing. Hold too long and you bin it and lose the chain.
  • Take the gold ring. The one against the rocks pays three times the safe one.
  • Keep moving. The chain now goes cold on a timer, not just on a miss.
  • Run the wall. Shaving rock or cliff at speed pays while you hold it.
